What the entry becomes

Averages are computed only from days that were actually filled in, so a missed day never drags them down. This is the same rule the API follows server-side.

The database also carries CHECK ("energyLevel" BETWEEN 1 AND 5) and the same for stress and sleep quality, so a future CSV import or device sync cannot store an out-of-range value even if a route forgets to validate.

Historical editing

The day picker steps back through the last 60 days. A day with no record opens blank rather than inheriting yesterday's numbers, and saving over a day replaces its record. Rows imported from a CSV keep their source label; only manual saves are relabelled.
